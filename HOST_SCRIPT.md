## 1. Opening — what this game is

*[Lobby or rosters on Monitor 1. You’re unmuted and smiling.]*

**Welcome, everyone! We’re playing Mega Family Feud.**

**If you’ve ever watched Family Feud — or Gimme 5 — you’ll recognize the vibe: a survey-style question, a board of hidden answers, and a team trying to uncover as many as they can.**

**But this is not exactly Family Feud. Here’s how we’re different:**

- **No face-off.** We don’t start with two people buzzing for control of the board.
- **No strikes, and no classic Family Feud steal of the whole board.** Every answer is worth the same: **one word.**
- **Score is simply: how many answers did your team reveal?** More words = better. (We do have a fun **STEAL** at the end of a turn if cards are left — I’ll explain that in a minute.)
- **Each team gets its own question and its own turn** — you play as a group, in order, against the clock.
- **I am the judge.** You say answers out loud. There’s no AI matching. If I think it counts, I flip the card. If it’s close, I’ll decide — and if I mess up, I can undo.

**Speed still matters.** We track when your team flips its last card. If two teams end with the same number of words, **the faster last word wins.**

**We’re going to do a short Trial Round first** — same mechanics as the real game, funny practice questions, so everyone sees how a turn works. Then we’ll reset and play for real.

*[Click **Trial Mode** on Host Control. Confirm the Trial banner shows on Monitor 1.]*

**Okay — we’re in Trial Mode. Your teams stay the same; we’re just using practice questions. Nothing here counts toward the real score.**

## 3. How a turn works (demo with Trial)

*[Pick Team 1. Say their name. Click **Start Game / Start Team**. Question + 10 cards + timer appear.]*

**When a team’s turn starts, you’ll see three things:**

1. **The survey question** — big and clear.
2. **Ten answer cards** — all hidden at first.
3. **A team clock** — that’s your turn limit. When it hits zero, the buzzer goes off and the turn is over. You’ll also hear a warning in the last 10 seconds.

**Your goal: reveal as many of those ten answers as you can before time runs out.**

### Who answers

**Players go in order — the order you see on screen.**  
**One person answers at a time.** When you’re done (hit or miss), we move to the next person. When we get back to the first person, that’s a new rotation — you can keep going until **time is up** or the board is full. There’s no “only two rounds” limit; the clock is the limit.

*[Optional: tap a player chip / Next Player so their name lights up.]*

**Optional: I may run a short 5-second buzz for pacing — that’s just for energy. The real limit is the team clock.**

### Guessing

**Say your answer out loud — clear enough for everyone to hear.**

**If it’s on the board, I’ll flip the card.** You’ll hear the flip. That counts as **one word** for your team.

**If it’s not on the board, we just move on. No strikes. You don’t lose points for a miss — you only lose time.**

**Close answers?** I’ll call it. If I flip the wrong card by mistake, I’ll undo it. Don’t worry.

*[During Trial: get 2–3 reveals so people see flips. Point at the answer count, e.g. “3 / 10 Answers.”]*

### Ending a turn

**A turn ends when:**

- **Time’s up**, or  
- **All ten answers are revealed**, or  
- **I end the turn early** (for example if you’re stuck and we need to keep the show moving).

**Your score for that round is the number of cards you flipped.**  
**We also save the time of your last flip** — that’s the tiebreaker if needed later.

## 3b. STEAL mode (manual — after a turn, if cards remain)

*[Do this after End Team Turn when the board still has hidden answers. Not built into the app — you run it from Teams chat + scoreboard. Skip on Trial if you want a shorter demo; use it in the real game.]*

**If time’s up and there are still answers left on the board, we open STEAL.**

**How STEAL works:**

1. **I call “STEAL!”** Everyone watches the Teams meeting chat — not the game window.
2. **Type this exact sentence in the chat** (punctuation and capitals must match):

   ```text
   We will miss you, Raf
   ```

   - Capital **W** on “We”  
   - Capital **R** on “Raf”  
   - Comma after “you,”  
   - No extra spaces, no period at the end, no emoji, no typos  

3. **First correct message wins the steal** — I shout their name. Everyone else stop typing.
4. That person gets **one guess** at **any remaining hidden answer** (say it out loud).
5. **If it’s on the board:** I flip the card and give **+1** to **their** team (Scoreboard → **+1** on that team — even if they weren’t the team that just played).
6. **If it’s wrong:** no point, steal is over, we move on.

**Only one steal attempt per turn** (one chat winner, one guess). Then **Next Team →**.

### What you say when opening STEAL

**Time’s up for [Team]! There are still answers left — STEAL is open!**

**First person to type this in the Teams chat — exact spelling, capitals, and comma — wins the steal:**

**We will miss you, Raf**

*[Watch chat. Confirm the first exact match.]*

**[Name] from Team [X] — you’ve got the steal! One guess. What’s still on that board?**

*[Correct → flip card + bump their team +1. Wrong → “Not up there — steal over.”]*

**Steal’s done. Next team!**

### Host tips (private)

- Keep Host Control’s answer list open so you can judge the steal guess quickly.
- Reject near-misses in chat (`we will miss you raf`, `We will miss you Raf`, trailing period, etc.) — exact only, or the race isn’t fair.
- If two messages land at once, the **earlier timestamp** in Teams wins.
- Award the point with Scoreboard **+1** on the stealer’s team (manual override is fine).
- After **End Team Turn**, **Score on flip** turns OFF automatically. Flip leftover cards for the
  audience — they show on Monitor 1 but do **not** add to that team’s score. Turn it back ON before
  the next team’s real play (Next Team does this for you).
- Optional: don’t run STEAL in Trial; save the hype for the real game.

*[Show summary briefly. Next Team if you want a second demo team, or skip to wrap the trial.]*

**That’s one full turn — including how STEAL works if cards are left. Every team gets the same kind of turn with their own question.**

**If two teams have the same number of words, the one with the faster last word — the smaller time — wins.**

## Live-game cue card (keep this open)

| Moment | You say / do |
| --- | --- |
| Start team | “Team ___, you’re up.” → **Start Team** |
| Player up | Name on screen; optional **Space** for 5s buzz |

| Show leftovers | Flip hidden cards while Score on flip is OFF (no points to that team) |

| Next | **Next Team →** (Score on flip back ON) |
| All done | **Show Results Board** → celebrate → **End Game** |

### Shortcuts (Host only)

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` | Start / pause 5s player buzz |
| `Enter` | Next player |
| `1`–`9`, `0` | Reveal / hide answers 1–10 |
| `U` | Undo last reveal |
| `R` | Jump to next rotation (first player) |
| `E` | End team turn |
| `S` | Toggle results board on Monitor 1 |

## STEAL cheat sheet (print / pin this)

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When | After a team’s turn ends **with hidden cards still on the board** |
| Where | Teams **meeting chat** (not the game app) |
| Trigger (exact) | `We will miss you, Raf` |
| Must have | Capital **W**, capital **R**af, comma after **you,** — nothing else |
| Winner | First exact message (earlier timestamp if tied) |
| Prize | **One** verbal guess at a remaining answer |
| Correct | Flip card + **+1** to **that person’s team** (Scoreboard) |
| Wrong | No point; steal ends |
| Cap | One steal per turn, then next team |

App note: STEAL is **manual** — flip the card yourself if you want it shown, and always award the point with Scoreboard **+1**.
